Walk Challenge - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Health data we access

With your permission, the Walk Challenge app reads your step count from Google Health Connect on Android (this includes steps recorded by your phone and a connected smartwatch). We request this access only so the app can count your steps automatically in walking challenges.

How we use it

  • Step counts are sent to our servers and added to your challenge totals and leaderboard position.
  • We use step data only to run the challenges you join. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for any other purpose.

What we do not collect

We do not read your location, heart rate, sleep, or any other health data. The app only requests step counts.

Revoking access

You can revoke step access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Walk Challenge → Permissions, or in the Health Connect app. If you revoke access, step sync stops working, but you can still enter steps manually on the web version.

Account data

When you create an account we store your Cash App $cashtag, display name, date of birth (to verify you are 18 or older), and your challenge activity (entries, step totals, and prizes). You must be 18 or older to use Walk Challenge.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the app operator through the Walk Challenge website.
